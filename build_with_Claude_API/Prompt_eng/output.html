# One-Day Meal Plan for Marathon Race Preparation

Here is a one-day carb-loading meal plan for an athlete preparing for a marathon:

* **Calorie Target:** Approximately 3,200 calories
* **Macronutrient Breakdown:** Protein (120g), Fat (85g), Carbs (520g)

## Meal Plan:

* **Breakfast (7:00 AM):** Oatmeal (100g dry weight) with banana (150g), honey (25g), and whole milk (250g). Toast with almond butter (20g).
    * Protein: 18g, Fat: 12g, Carbs: 95g

* **Mid-Morning Snack (10:00 AM):** Bagel (90g) with jam (30g) and low-fat cream cheese (20g). Orange juice (250ml).
    * Protein: 10g, Fat: 5g, Carbs: 75g

* **Lunch (1:00 PM):** Pasta (120g cooked) with lean ground turkey (150g), marinara sauce (150g), and grated Parmesan (15g). Side salad with olive oil dressing (15ml).
    * Protein: 38g, Fat: 15g, Carbs: 85g

* **Afternoon Snack (4:00 PM):** Rice cakes (40g) with peanut butter (25g) and honey (20g). Apple (180g).
    * Protein: 10g, Fat: 12g, Carbs: 80g

* **Dinner (7:00 PM):** Grilled chicken breast (160g) with white rice (150g cooked), steamed carrots (150g), and olive oil (10ml).
    * Protein: 32g, Fat: 15g, Carbs: 95g

* **Evening Snack (9:30 PM):** Sports drink (500ml) with a granola bar (50g).
    * Protein: 12g, Fat: 8g, Carbs: 90g

This meal plan emphasizes high-quality carbohydrates to maximize glycogen storage, includes adequate protein for muscle recovery and repair, and provides sufficient calories to fuel endurance training and race preparation.
